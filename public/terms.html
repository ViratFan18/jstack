Terms of Use

Plain-language information about using the JStack website and browser-based tools. This page does not identify a governing law or replace legal advice.

Using the website

You may use the available tools and learning material for development and educational workflows. You are responsible for deciding whether a tool is appropriate, checking its output, and complying with requirements that apply to your work. No account is required.

Tool limits and security

JWT decoding only reads encoded content; it does not verify a signature. Signature and claim verification is not authorization. JSON syntax checks do not validate schemas or business rules. Base64 is not encryption, and JStack Base128 is a custom format. Generated Java is inferred from sample input. Review these limits in the Developer Tool Disclaimer before relying on output.

Inputs, preferences, and feedback

Tool operations run in your browser and do not require an account or a JStack application backend. Optional theme, recent-tool, and favorite-tool preferences may be stored locally in your browser. The feedback page prepares a mailto link; the visitor chooses whether to open and send it through their email provider. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Changes and availability

Tools, pages, and supported formats may change, and the website may be unavailable at times. Check the current page and test outputs in your own environment before relying on them.

Contact
